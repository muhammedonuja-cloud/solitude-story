A gentle reminder

Dear Obom,

You do not need to become someone else to deserve love. You do not have to be perfect to be beautiful. You do not need everyone's approval to know your worth.

Speak to yourself with tenderness. Celebrate the little things you survive. Forgive yourself for the days you feel lost. Let your heart rest when it needs rest.

You are a work in progress, not a work without value.

Before you leave

I am allowed to grow slowly.
I am allowed to begin again.
I am allowed to choose myself.
I am worthy of the love I give away.

With gentleness, from me to me.
